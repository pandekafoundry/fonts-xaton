Xaton is a contemporary sans-serif typeface designed by Pandeka Foundry. Xaton is a bold geometric display typeface built from sharp, interlocking forms. Diagonal cuts, flat terminals, and split counters give every letter a constructed, architectural presence, combining angular strokes, tight apertures, and confident structural weight to create a striking visual identity. .

With clean geometry and refined proportions, Xaton is well-suited for branding, editorial, digital interfaces, and any project that demands a modern, professional typographic presence.
